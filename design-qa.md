## Findings and corrections

1. **P2 — Initial control and caption type was too small relative to the selected visual.** Increased desktop metadata, planner headings, dates, effort labels, explanation text, action type and graph labels. Retained more compact controls at short laptop heights. The final source-size full and detail comparisons show the corrected hierarchy.
2. **P2 — Plot library inferred an unwanted “id” axis label and short baseline marks collided.** Suppressed the inferred axis label and removed the conflicting tick layer. Baseline and current points now share real heart-rate coordinates. Labels distinguish their values.
3. **P2 — Mobile evidence-first stacking delayed the next action.** Changed the grid areas and DOM order to headline → planner → evidence on narrow screens. Replaced the compact desktop plot/table with semantic workload summaries. At 390 × 844, labels and units remain intact and document width does not exceed the viewport.
4. **P2 — A simple cumulative progress indicator could falsely mark collection complete when symptoms bypass it.** Added a tested progress adapter. The direct-review path marks planning and collection skipped; collection becomes complete only after a linked observation exists. Two regression tests cover this boundary and the normal collection flow.
5. **P2 — Prepared snapshot freshness was previously unclear.** Added a readable preview and a freshness comparison against current observations, baseline, opportunities, history and evaluation. Changed evidence marks the snapshot stale; an actual manual response does not falsely make unchanged evidence stale. Downloads still use the exact prepared packet.

No outstanding P0, P1 or P2 visual findings remain in the checked scope.

## Intentional deviations from the generated reference

- Corrected its inaccurate point spacing: 107 bpm is close to the 105–106 bpm baseline on the same axis. A missing value has no plotted numerical point.
- Retained engine-withheld comparisons when evidence is incompatible; showing a baseline graph must not imply compatibility.
- Used dark text on orange primary buttons for stronger contrast than the mock's small white labels.
- Added readable value details, original records, genuine planner reasons, imports, review preparation and response entry because the selected still image does not cover the full working product.
- Removed the nonfunctional decorative ruler. Kept actual chart ticks and the instrumentation treatment.
- Used a compact variant for short laptop viewports and stacked workload readouts on mobile.

## Functional verification

Through the in-app Browser:

- Turned off the first prescribed opportunity; selection moved to the second opportunity and its modelled effort.
- Accepted the new plan, refreshed and retained the accepted state.
- Generated a new synthetic session; its 100 W value was **145 bpm**. The original incomplete session and two frozen baseline sessions remained present.
- Prepared a readable review, downloaded the actual JSON and inspected its `prepared-locally` transfer state, simulated source, two baseline sessions and two linked history entries.
- Recorded an explicitly synthetic manual response and observed it in the interface.
- Exercised no-opportunity, incompatible-protocol and symptoms scenarios. No opportunity left the missing HR explicit; incompatible records withheld comparison; symptoms hid collection opportunities and offered review.
- Checked NASA post-bed-rest 100 W evidence at **166 bpm** against **124–125 bpm**, then recovery at **139 bpm** and **+14.5 bpm**. No prospective planner controls appeared in the research view.
- Checked body-map navigation, formal VO₂ tools and legacy replay. Research showed **73.9%** mass-normalized retention. Switching to the separately recorded absolute series preserved its **2.725 L/min** baseline and distinct **74.9%** result. No research amber determination appeared.
- Checked source-size desktop, 1280 × 720 laptop and 390 × 844 narrow layouts; the checked laptop and mobile document widths did not overflow horizontally.
- Tested keyboard Space on a focused availability switch and observed the selection and its live status return to the first opportunity. Focus styles, labelled controls and reduced-motion handling exist; a complete assistive-technology audit was not performed.
- Browser console inspection returned no warnings or errors at the end of the checked flows.
